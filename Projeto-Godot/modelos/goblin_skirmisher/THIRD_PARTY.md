# Generation provenance

The character and dagger meshes, textures and humanoid rig were generated using a paid AI asset service. Included motions are retargeted stock actions. They are supplied together with the character, not as a standalone motion library. Exact stock action IDs and names appear in Documentation/animation-catalog.json. Reference images were AI-generated.

G4G Assets performed local conversion, equipment attachment and quality checks. The supplied buyer license applies across free and source tiers. No third-party marketing music or dungeon scenery is included.
